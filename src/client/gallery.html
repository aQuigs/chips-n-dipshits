Chips n’ Dipshits · design options

Pick a look

Four looks for the table, A to D. All four ship with the app and each player can switch in the menu, so your pick only decides what everyone sees first. The two screens below are the real app playing a live hand against five bots, and it is your turn.

What changes between looks

  • Card Room and Dive Bar use a classic two-color deck; After Hours and Daylight use four colors, so clubs and spades are never confused.
  • Daylight is the only light one, for playing outside or on a dim phone.
  • Layout, buttons, chat and menus are the same in all four.

Things to try

  • Call or raise: you hold top pair with the nut flush draw, and the bots play on after you.
  • Open the menu (☰) for the ledger, hand history, table settings and invite links. You are the host here.
  • When a bot goes broke it asks to buy back in, and the request lands with you to approve.

About this preview

  • The table and the bots live in this page. Nothing here touches Google or is saved.
  • Both screens are the same seat, the way it looks if you carry on from your phone.
  • The action clock is off here so the hand waits for you. Real tables default to 30 seconds.
